**The rule behind "bold":** one idea per frame, at full scale and full contrast, and every idea is a fact the site already publishes (read-only access, evidence behind every finding, the automated tests before every update, the seat count). The attention it earns rests on proof, never hype. No invented results, no client names, no fake urgency beyond the real 50-seat cap.

## Safe zones and language

- Vertical frames keep everything between 250 px from the top and 1440 px (TikTok/Reels captions and buttons below), and 150 px clear of the right edge, where TikTok's buttons sit in **both** languages.
- Text-on-image exists in AR and EN versions; `before-after_neutral_*` carries no words. Numbers and source chips are Latin digits in both languages, as the site does.
- Carousels advance by swiping left in both languages; the Arabic cue is «اسحب ←».
