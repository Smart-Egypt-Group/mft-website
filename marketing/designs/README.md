# Launch designs (SEG Audit Founding Members)

> **⛔ تحديث 6 نوفمبر 2026:** كل تصميم يعرض «33/50» أو «انضمت 17» أو مقاعد ممتلئة **مسحوب** (seats، neutral-close، hemicycle، hourglass، psych-4، founder-pov، إطارات ختام الفيديو، غلاف highlight 02). الندرة الآن: نافذة إغلاق سعر المؤسِّس + انتقائية بالطلب. القائمة والمواصفات: [`marketing/strategy/design-redo-list.md`](../strategy/design-redo-list.md).

Generated by [`scripts/launch-images.js`](../../scripts/launch-images.js) in the blog's brand system: navy `#0D1B3E`, cyan `#1BB7CD`, brilliant pure white, no gold, golden-ratio split, small MFT mark. Every design exists as **`.webp`** (web, smaller) and **`.jpg`** (upload to platforms that reject WebP: Meta Ads, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, TikTok). Open [`index.html`](index.html) locally to review the whole set.

**The visual:** the 50-seat grid. All 50 are open rings and the visitor's seat glows cyan (filled seats appear only if the real counter is switched on), reached by the cyan line from the seam of light. The "now live" set uses a ledger with one cyan line and a check. The "neutral" files carry no words so one file serves both languages.

**No member count on any design (6 Nov 2026 quality pass).** The campaign designs (`close-*`) carry the closing window from `src/site.config.js → founding.closesAt` («30 نوفمبر · سعر المؤسِّس يُغلق نهائيًا») and selectivity («العضوية بالطلب، ومش كل طلب بيتقبل»); all copy comes from `scripts/lib/campaign.js`. No seat numbers, no numeric price, no «مدى الحياة». Every design carries the platform signature (mark + SEG + BY MFT). `node scripts/design-qa.js` checks copy, withdrawn files and platform sizes. The counter variant exists in the generator and appears only when `src/site.config.js → founding.showCount` is turned on with a confirmed count. Covers and banners deliberately carry no seat count (they live for months).

**Safe zones:** stories/TikTok keep copy between 250 px from the top and 480–520 px from the bottom, and 150–170 px from the end edge (TikTok buttons). Covers keep copy clear of the profile-picture corner. The YouTube banner keeps everything inside the 1546×423 area that every device shows.

**Creative concepts** (hook 360, self-audit, before/after, zero guesswork, hemicycle, carousel, short-video frames, founder POV overlay) live in [`concepts/`](concepts/README.md), generated by `scripts/launch-concepts.js`.

The **persuasion pack** (loss aversion, curiosity gap, shocking specificity, scarcity + proof, meta authority, anchoring, pattern break, the hawk’s eye) lives in [`psych/`](psych/README.md), generated by `scripts/psych-ads.js`.

The **guerrilla "attention" pack** lives in [`guerrilla/`](guerrilla/README.md), generated by `scripts/guerrilla-ads.js`. It has three stylized styles (graffiti on a wall, writing on the palm, a sticky note on a screen), each in square and story.
