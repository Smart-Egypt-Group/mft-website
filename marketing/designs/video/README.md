# SEG Audit: Reels / Shorts

Vertical videos for Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Generated by [`scripts/reels-videos.js`](../../../scripts/reels-videos.js) from the storyboard frames in [`../concepts/video/`](../concepts/video/).

**Specs:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, MP4, H.264 video, AAC audio (silent: 48 kHz stereo).

| File | Storyboard | Length |
|---|---|---|
| `reel-a_ar_1080x1920.mp4` | «باقي 360 يوم» (frames 1–5 + end card) | 22.0 s |
| `reel-a_en_1080x1920.mp4` | "The other 360 days" | 22.0 s |
| `reel-b_ar_1080x1920.mp4` | «قبل / بعد» (frames 1–4 + end card) | 18.4 s |
| `reel-b_en_1080x1920.mp4` | "Before / After" | 18.4 s |

**Motion:**
- Every frame enters with a soft wipe in the reading direction (right to left in Arabic), so its text appears progressively, then holds with a slow push-in.
- The end card reveals «فحص مجاني لميزان شركتك في دقائق · seg-audit.com» word by word, with the MFT mark.

**Data rule:** frames that show ledger figures carry «مثال توضيحي · داتا اختبارية». The figures are design placeholders, not MFT or client data.

**Audio:** the track is silent, so there is nothing to license. Add music in each platform's own editor, from its commercial library.

**Public copies:** identical files are served at `https://www.seg-audit.com/assets/social/video/<file>`, for posting by URL (for example, Instagram Reels `video_url`).

**Regenerate** after any change to the frames or the seat count:
1. `node scripts/launch-concepts.js`
2. `node scripts/reels-videos.js`
