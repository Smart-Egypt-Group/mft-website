# Standard Enterprise Governance (SEG): Reels / Shorts, cinematic cut

Vertical videos for Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. They are generated by [`scripts/reels-cinematic.js`](../../../scripts/reels-cinematic.js), with the soundtrack from [`scripts/reels-audio.js`](../../../scripts/reels-audio.js). They replace the earlier `reel-a` / `reel-b` cuts.

**Specs:**
- 1080×1920, 30 fps, 34 s.
- Video: H.264 High profile, 10 Mbps.
- Audio: AAC 48 kHz stereo at 192 kbps, normalised to -16 LUFS integrated (EBU R128 gating), with peaks capped at -1.5 dBFS.

## The six scenarios (Arabic `_ar` and English `_en`)

| File | Scenario | Platform check it shows (seg-audit `audit-engine/app/analysis`) |
|---|---|---|
| `reel-duplicate-payment_*` | The same supplier bill paid twice | `red_flags.duplicate_payments` (Critical) |
| `reel-post-close-entry_*` | An entry posted after the period close | `je_closing` `JE_POST_CLOSE` / `JE_BACKDATED` (5 days) |
| `reel-suspense-account_*` | A suspense account that keeps growing | `standards.uncleared_suspense` / `accounting_treatment` |
| `reel-ghost-vendor_*` | A supplier bank account that matches an employee's | `red_flags` `GHOST_VENDOR` (Critical; needs the Employees app). Worded as an indicator to review, never an accusation |
| `reel-round-manual_*` | Round manual entries in the last two days of the month | `gl_integrity` (round amounts, manual entries) |
| `reel-no-receipt_*` | Approved expense claims with no attachment | `expenses` (no `message_main_attachment_id`) |

## Story structure (every reel)

| Seconds | Section |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | The pain, with the key figure landing on an impact |
| 5–12 | Why the annual audit misses it |
| 12–24 | Standard Enterprise Governance flags it at once: the evidence card builds, the camera zooms onto the matched line, the matched fields light up, then the indicator chips, the severity and the evidence note |
| 24–29 | The control that fixes it |
| 29–34 | End card: «Standard Enterprise Governance — المنصة التي تراجع نفسها», the free-check CTA, seg-audit.com and the MFT mark |

A chapter bar runs on top (المشكلة · ليه بتعدّي · إزاي بتتكشف · الضابط).

## Motion

- Expo and spring easing.
- Parallax background layers: grid, two glow orbs, grain and vignette.
- A slow camera drift.
- Word-by-word kinetic type.
- Soft-focus scene transitions, with a light sweep in the reading direction (right to left in Arabic).

## Data rule

Every name, code and figure is test data on a fictional model company. «مثال توضيحي · داتا اختبارية» stays on screen, and the evidence card carries a watermark. No MFT or client data.

## Audio and licensing

**No third-party audio is used.** There is no music, sample, loop or recording, so there is nothing to license.

Every sound is synthesised by `scripts/reels-audio.js` from sine waves and white noise:

| Sound | How it is made |
|---|---|
| Music bed | A calm ambient pad on Am(add9), Fmaj7, C(add9), G6, with a quiet shimmer |
| Whoosh | Band-passed noise sweep on each scene change, panned in the reading direction |
| Tick | A soft two-partial ping on each revealed finding and point |
| Impact | A sub drop with a noise thump on the key figure |
| Resolve | An A major add9 bell chord on the call to action |

The music ducks up to -6 dB under the effects.

## Encoding

- ffmpeg is not available on this machine: Homebrew has no bottles for macOS 14, and the Command Line Tools are not installed.
- Encoding therefore uses Chrome WebCodecs (H.264 + AAC), and the MP4 container is written by `scripts/tiktok-videos.js` → `mp4()`.

## Where the files are

- **Committed and published:** `src/assets/social/video/<file>`, served at `https://www.seg-audit.com/assets/social/video/<file>`.
- **Local render output:** this folder holds identical copies after a render. They are git-ignored so the ~280 MB set is not committed twice.
