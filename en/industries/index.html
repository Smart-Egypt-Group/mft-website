Trading & Distribution

Margin by SKU, customer and channel, after discounts, returns and freight.

ISIC divisions: 45, 46

From the classification list: Wholesale trade: distributors, wholesale agents and importers; Vehicle trade, spare parts and service centres

What usually goes wrong

  • Gross margin reported before rebates, returns and logistics
  • Stock in transit and consignment stock outside the system
  • Duplicate and near-duplicate vendors paid twice for months

How MFT helps

  • Odoo Sales, Purchase and Inventory with landed-cost allocation
  • Vendor-master, receivables ageing and credit-limit controls
  • Channel and customer profitability in the monthly pack

What SEG Audit checks in Odoo

  • Inventory valued automatically with every move
  • Three-way match: purchase order, receipt and bill
  • Freight and customs costs loaded onto inventory

Retail & Point of Sale

Recipe costing, expiry-driven stock and multi-outlet control.

ISIC divisions: 47, 56

From the classification list: Shops, chains, supermarkets and online stores; Restaurants, cafes and catering

What usually goes wrong

  • Recipe costs drifting with ingredient prices and portion sizes
  • Waste and expiry losses recorded late or not at all
  • Cash and stock controls across branches and central kitchens

How MFT helps

  • Lot and expiry tracking with recipe (BoM) costing in Odoo
  • Branch-level P&L with waste and variance reporting
  • Cash, purchasing and supplier-payment controls audited on site

What SEG Audit checks in Odoo

  • Inventory valued automatically with every move
  • Three-way match: purchase order, receipt and bill
  • Point-of-sale sessions closed the same day

Manufacturing

True product cost, from bill of materials to landed overhead.

ISIC divisions: 10 to 33 (section C)

From the classification list: Food products and beverages; Chemicals, metals, textiles, furniture and other manufacturing

What usually goes wrong

  • Standard costs that nobody has updated since the bill of materials changed
  • Work-in-progress that never reconciles to the ledger
  • Scrap, rework and yield losses invisible in the margin report

How MFT helps

  • Odoo Manufacturing with routings, work centres and actual costing
  • Inventory valuation and WIP controls tested by internal audit
  • Monthly product-margin reporting by line and customer

What SEG Audit checks in Odoo

  • Inventory valued automatically with every move
  • Active bills of materials for manufactured products
  • Work orders on manufacturing orders
  • A separate work-in-progress account

Services & Projects

Cost per project and per service line, from timesheets to retentions.

ISIC divisions: 41 to 43, 55, 58 to 63, 69 to 75, 77 to 82, 85 to 88

From the classification list: Building, civil engineering and specialised construction; Hospitals, clinics, radiology centres and laboratories

What usually goes wrong

  • Cost-to-complete and margin per project known only at the end
  • Retentions, advances and subcontractor certificates tracked in spreadsheets
  • Claims and insurer receivables reconciled months late

How MFT helps

  • Project-based analytic accounting and budgets in Odoo
  • Subcontractor, retention and progress-billing controls
  • Odoo configured for service-line accounting and insurer billing

What SEG Audit checks in Odoo

  • Timesheets on projects
  • Analytic distribution tying income and expenses to each project’s cost

Frequently asked

Which industries does MFT serve?

We serve activities classified under ISIC Rev.4 across four families: trading (45 and 46), retail and point of sale (47 and 56), manufacturing (10 to 33), and services and projects, including construction (41 to 43) and healthcare (86), in Egypt and Saudi Arabia. These are the families SEG Audit has Odoo control rules for today.

Do you configure Odoo differently per activity?

Yes. Manufacturing gets routings, work centres and actual costing; trading gets landed costs and credit controls; retail and restaurants get lot and expiry tracking with recipe costing and branch profitability; services and projects get project analytic accounting, retentions and service-line accounting. SEG Audit checks each family’s controls in a dedicated axis.

My activity is outside the four families. Can you still help?

SEG Audit’s general checks run on any activity. The activity-controls axis applies to the four families only, and we do not guess your activity from your data: the report states that your activity is outside the rules written today. Implementation and audit scope for any other activity is set on a call.
