Partners

Your clients run Odoo. Add continuous audit to your service.

The partner programme of Standard Enterprise Governance (SEG) from MFT, with its continuous audit product SEG Audit, for accounting and audit firms and Odoo implementation partners in Egypt and Saudi Arabia. Give your clients continuous review of their books: read-only access, every finding with its evidence, and approval by a named MFT reviewer before release.

Become a partner

Value for your firm

Review your clients faster. The judgement stays yours.

  1. The platform prepares, you judge

    388 checks across 22 reports on each company’s books, with findings ranked by severity and each one tied to its evidence. You read the ten that matter instead of thousands of entries, and the professional judgement stays yours.

  2. All your clients from one account

    The Enterprise plan covers up to 25 companies and 100 reports a month on one account, with the same method for every client.

  3. A named reviewer on every report

    Every report is approved by a named MFT reviewer before it is released, and your firm reads it with your client.

  4. A recurring monthly service

    You read the approved report with your client before every close. A monthly relationship instead of a yearly visit.

Who it is for

Three kinds of partner.

  1. Odoo implementation partners

    Add continuous audit to implementation and upgrade projects, so your client knows the ledger is right after go-live, not only on hand-over day.

  2. Accounting and audit firms

    Offer your Odoo clients a monthly review of their books before close, with an Arabic report that ties every finding to its source.

  3. Independent advisers and fractional CFOs

    Read every client’s books with the same method, from one account, instead of a manual review that differs from company to company.

Three tracks

Choose the way of working that fits your firm.

  1. Referral

    Introduce a client; we sell, invoice and serve, and you earn a commission on collected revenue.

  2. Reseller

    Sell and invoice yourself at the partner price, and give your clients first-line support.

  3. Practice partner

    Offer continuous audit as a monthly service from your firm, from one account for up to 25 companies, and read the reports with your clients before every close.

What partners get

What is available today.

One account, many companies
The Enterprise plan covers up to 25 companies and 100 reports a month on one account, so you run your clients from one place.
388 checks across 22 reports
Duplicate payments, near-duplicate vendors, approvals and segregation of duties, cut-off, inventory valuation, tax and e-invoicing (ETA and ZATCA), and the financial statements.
A free trial-balance check for each client company
Every company starts with a free check of its trial balance that flags its risk points in minutes, so your client sees the value on their own numbers before any commitment.
A read-only connector module
The seg_audit_connector module is open source (LGPL-3) and runs on Odoo 17, 18 and 19: a dedicated read-only user, and a button that revokes access at once.
A named reviewer on every report
Every report stays a draft until an MFT reviewer approves it by name. Reports support decisions; they are not a formal audit opinion.

Activity packages

Each activity has its own controls in Odoo.

The platform classifies the client’s activity (ISIC Rev.4) and then checks the controls that activity needs in its Odoo setup, on top of the general checks.

  1. Trading and distribution

    Wholesale and distribution

    • Inventory valued automatically with every move
    • Three-way match: purchase order, receipt and bill
    • Freight and customs costs loaded onto inventory

  2. Retail and point of sale

    Shops, restaurants and points of sale

    • Inventory valued automatically with every move
    • Three-way match: purchase order, receipt and bill
    • Point-of-sale sessions closed the same day

  3. Manufacturing

    Manufacturing, including food and beverage

    • Inventory valued automatically with every move
    • Active bills of materials for manufactured products
    • Work orders on manufacturing orders
    • A separate work-in-progress account

  4. Services and projects

    Contracting, healthcare and professional services

    • Timesheets on projects
    • Analytic distribution tying income and expenses to each project’s cost

SEG Audit checks the controls above today on any Odoo system. Ready-made module packages for each activity are being built and will be listed on the Odoo App Store soon; until then they are set up within the implementation project.

If you are the client’s external auditor

To protect your independence: referral only, with no commission on that client, and the client contracts with MFT directly. For clients whose statements you do not audit, you can be a reseller or a practice partner. Every partner agreement applies this limit.
