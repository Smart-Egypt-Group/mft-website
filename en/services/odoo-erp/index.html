Odoo ERP Solutions

An ERP that mirrors the business, closes the books, and governs itself.

Overview

Odoo ERP Solutions by MFT is one service for the whole life of the system: implementation and configuration, integrations and custom reports, custom module development, training, support and upgrades, and tax inside Odoo. It is delivered by Odoo implementation experts led by accountants, so the ledger is right from day one and stays right.

40+

40+ review axes on your books, one of them for your activity family’s controls

With SEG Audit, we classify your activity under ISIC Rev.4 and check your books across 40+ review axes, including one dedicated to your activity family’s controls inside Odoo (trading, retail, manufacturing, services). We configure those controls into your system from the first setup. This is what Standard Enterprise Governance means inside your system: governance is part of the configuration, not a report that comes after it.

Odoo implementations fail when the chart of accounts, cost centres and tax setup are an afterthought. MFT starts there. Our Odoo implementation experts, led by accountants, configure finance, inventory, manufacturing, sales, purchasing and HR around how your operation actually runs, for industrial, trading and service companies in Egypt and the Gulf.

Because we practise smart audit, permissions, segregation of duties and documentation cycles are designed into the system from the first configuration, so the ERP prevents errors instead of just recording them. After go-live the same team stays with you: it trains every role, supports and upgrades the system, and builds what your activity needs.

Frequently asked

How long does an implementation take?

Six weeks for any implementation project, from discovery to go-live.

Do you have ready-made modules for each activity?

Odoo module packages for each activity, with its controls, are being built and will be listed on the Odoo App Store soon; until then we configure your activity’s controls and build what is needed within the implementation project. Today, with SEG Audit, we classify your activity under ISIC Rev.4 and check your books across 40+ review axes, including one dedicated to your activity family’s controls. Sector benchmarks are planned once enough classified clients are on the platform.

Do you handle Egyptian e-invoicing and Saudi ZATCA?

Yes. ETA e-invoicing, ZATCA phase two, withholding tax and multi-entity consolidation are part of the standard build. For return reconciliation and tax-file readiness, see Innovative Tax Solutions.

What happens after go-live?

Ninety days of stabilisation are included, then optional support with response times agreed per contract; critical issues are acknowledged the same business day.

Do you handle upgrades and integrations?

Yes. Version upgrades with regression testing and a monthly health check, and integrations with banks, e-invoicing portals (ETA, ZATCA), WhatsApp and third-party tools via API.

Is training on site or remote, and is competency verified?

Either. Sessions are recorded so new hires onboard without a new engagement, and check-outs before go-live confirm each role can complete its daily tasks.

We are an Odoo partner serving several clients. Does SEG work for us?

Yes. Each client lives in its own organisation, isolated from every other one, and that isolation was verified on all seven organisations at backfill, is probed live in production, and is covered by automated tests. Talk to us about a partner arrangement and how many clients you serve.
