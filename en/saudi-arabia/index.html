Saudi Arabia

ZATCA-ready Odoo, audit and Virtual CFO for Saudi companies.

MFT serves Saudi companies with ZATCA phase-two compliant Odoo ERP implementation, internal audit programmes and Virtual CFO reporting, delivered on site and remotely in Arabic and English.

Saudi finance teams face ZATCA phase-two integration, 15% VAT, zakat and withholding rules, and a data-protection law that decides where client data may live. MFT builds all of that into the implementation and the audit programme, and its own tax and zakat research covers the Egypt and Saudi treaty position for groups operating in both.

What compliance looks like here

  • ZATCA e-invoicing phase two: integration, clearance and reporting of invoices
  • VAT at 15%, zakat for Saudi and GCC-owned entities, and withholding on payments abroad
  • SOCPA standards and the annual external audit
  • Personal Data Protection Law: rules on processing personal data and transferring it outside the Kingdom

How engagements run

  2. 02A free check of your company’s trial balance that flags its risk points in minutes
  3. 03Read-only access to your books; we change nothing in your system
  4. 04Reports written for owners, boards and Saudi auditors

Questions from this market

Is your Odoo implementation ZATCA phase-two compliant?

Yes. Integration, clearance and reporting are part of the standard implementation, and the configuration is tested against the ZATCA sandbox before go-live.

Where does my data live?

Your ledger stays on your own Odoo or ERP, and we read it read-only. Files you upload to SEG are processed on the SEG platform server, and during the current phase MFT Intelligence agent runs are processed by a cloud AI model provider. Ask us for the processing details and have your legal adviser review them against the Personal Data Protection Law before you connect live data.

Do you have a Saudi office?

Engagements are delivered on site in Saudi Arabia and remotely from Cairo. Fieldwork visits are scheduled as part of the engagement.
