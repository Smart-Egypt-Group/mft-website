Privacy

What we collect, and why.

This website is a marketing site. It collects only what you type into the contact form.

What we collect

When you submit the contact form we receive the name, company, email, phone number, country, service of interest and message you enter, plus the page you sent it from and the language you used. The site sets no tracking cookies and uses no third-party analytics.

Why we collect it

To reply to your request, prepare your first report and, if you become a client, to run the engagement. Your details are recorded in MFT’s customer system and are not sold, rented or shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Financial data you send us

Any ledger, trial balance or report you share for a first report is treated as confidential client data: read-only access, restricted to the engagement team, and deleted on request once the engagement ends.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted at any time by emailing info@seg-audit.com. We answer within one business day.

Other MFT platforms

The seg-audit platform (app.seg-audit.com) has its own terms and privacy policy, which apply when you create an account there.
