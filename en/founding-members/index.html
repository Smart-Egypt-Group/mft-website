The free check

A free check of your company’s trial balance that flags its risk points in minutes

Not a full audit report: computed figures, not estimates. An uploaded trial balance gives the balance checks; the four entry-level checks run when Odoo is connected read-only.

  1. 01

    Suspense accounts

    Balances left in clearing or suspense accounts (Odoo connected).

  2. 02

    Abnormal balances

    An account with a balance against its nature, such as cash in credit (Odoo connected).

  3. 03

    Imbalance

    Total debits do not equal total credits.

  4. 04

    Heavy manual entries

    A high share of manual entries on sensitive accounts (Odoo connected).

  5. 05

    Backdated entries

    Entries dated earlier than the day they were recorded (Odoo connected).

How to join

Three steps to the risk points in your trial balance, in minutes.

    Sign up

  2. 02

    Upload your trial balance

    Upload the trial balance of your last closed month, or connect Odoo read-only.

Frequently asked

Why only 50 seats?

Every Founding Member works directly with the MFT team on setup and follow-up, and that does not stretch beyond 50 companies in the first phase.

What happens once the 50 seats are filled?

The programme closes. The platform stays open for sign-up, without the founder price.

What does “Founder price: 50% off for life” mean?

50% off any plan you choose, monthly or annual, applied at every renewal for as long as your subscription renews; you can cancel by yourself at any time. It is not offered once the seats are taken.

Can the platform change my data?

No. It reads only. The Odoo connection is read-only and the platform writes nothing to your system.

We are not on Odoo. Can we join?

Yes. Send your last closed month as a trial balance or spreadsheet and we work from that with you.
