Founding membership application

Apply to join the Founding Members

Two minutes of your time. The MFT team reviews every application by hand, and not every one is accepted. If yours is, you receive the acceptance letter with the founder price, then sign up on the platform with the same e-mail you applied with. Acceptance stays valid for 14 days; if you have not signed up and connected Odoo by then, it lapses.
